100% Pure

Nothing added. Nothing taken away.

Our bees forage the wildflowers of south Louisiana, and what they bring back is what goes in the jar. We don't heat it down or filter out the good parts — you get honey the way it comes out of the comb, with all its local character intact.

Because it's raw and local, no two harvests taste exactly alike. That's the point.

Good to know

More than a sweetener.

A Louisiana family farm

From our hives to your table.

Bayou Bees is part of Bayouland Ag Enterprises, the Corder family's farm in Crowley. No middleman, no mystery — just honey you can trace back to the hands that pulled it.

Step 01

We keep the hives

We tend the bees and pull the honey ourselves. Nothing gets outsourced, and nothing leaves the farm we wouldn't put on our own table.

Step 02

You order direct

Tell us what you'd like right here on the site. Your request comes straight to us — no store shelves in between.

Step 03

Pick up or delivery

We confirm your order and arrange local pickup in Crowley or delivery, so it reaches you fresh from the hive.

Good honey takes time. We've got the time.
